To install Werewolf Undercover, use the official listing for your phone: [Google Play for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=xyz.bubbleappstudio.werewolfundercover) or [the App Store for iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6740009341). You do not need to find a separate APK file to install the game through Google Play.

This guide covers Bubble App Studio’s in-person Werewolf game, with a phone guiding the narration. If you are planning a game night with friends, start by installing it on the phone you will use at the table.

## Android: start with the Google Play listing

1. Open the Google Play link above on your Android phone.
2. Check the developer: **Grégoire Lemoulant (Bubble App Studio)**. Similar app names can refer to different games.
3. Review device compatibility and purchase information, then use the installation option available for your device.
4. Open the app before your friends arrive so you can read its instructions.

Searching for a Werewolf APK may bring up several unrelated games. The direct store link identifies this app more clearly than its name alone. This guide links to Google Play; it does not provide an APK for manual installation.

## iPhone: use the official App Store page

Open the App Store link on the iPhone you intend to use. Check that the developer is **Gregoire Lemoulant** and review the compatibility section for the required iOS version. Follow the App Store’s instructions to download the app.

Sending the game to a friend who is hosting? Share the link for their phone. You can also send them [the game’s home page](/en/), which includes both store links and a preview of the app.

## Does a free download include everything?

The iPhone listing offers a free download with in-app purchases; Google Play also lists in-app purchases. Read the available options and displayed price before confirming a purchase. Downloading the app and buying optional content are separate steps. [Apple’s guide to in-app purchases](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102383) explains how to identify them on an App Store page.

## What if the app will not install?

On Android, first check your Internet connection and available storage, as recommended in [Google Play’s download troubleshooting guide](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/14122894?hl=en). If the store reports that the app is incompatible, read the message carefully: availability can depend on your device, operating system or country. [Google explains device compatibility here](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2851648?hl=en).

For help with the game itself, use the support contact on its listing and include your phone model, operating system version and the exact error message. Those details make a problem easier to investigate than “it does not work.”
