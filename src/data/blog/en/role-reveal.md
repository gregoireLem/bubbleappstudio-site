**Yes, under the Pact’s general rules, you can claim a role during discussion, truthfully or as a bluff. You cannot show your card.** The [rulebook, page 6, in French](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-asmodeeca/uploads/2023/07/WerewolvesThePact_FR_Rules.pdf#page=6), distinguishes speech from physical proof. Check any special rules for your characters; for another edition or an app, follow its own instructions.

## What does “revealing your role” mean?

Players may use that phrase for different actions. Before dealing roles, give the group an example of each so everyone understands the distinction.

| Situation | What the group receives |
| --- | --- |
| Maya says, “I’m a Villager.” | A claim to assess during discussion. |
| Maya describes her previous vote. | A public event other players may remember. |
| Maya turns over her card or shows a private screen. | Direct information that could remove uncertainty. The rules must specifically allow this action. |

Sounding confident does not turn the first statement into proof. Equally, declining to name your role is not enough to establish that you are a wolf.

## An example: continue the discussion without demanding cards

Maya claims to be a Villager, then asks Paul to show his card “just to be sure.” The moderator can remind the whole table of the rule without confirming or denying Maya’s claim. The moderator settles the procedure, not which player sounds believable.

To move the discussion forward, ask about a visible choice: “Paul, you supported Nora before the vote, then voted against her. What changed your mind?” Paul can answer without proving his identity. The table gets an explanation it can compare with the earlier conversation.

Avoid turning the debate into a compulsory round of role claims, too. That may pressure newcomers into sharing information they wanted to keep private. If your question is when to share a discovery, our [Seer guide](/en/blog/werewolf-seer-role-strategy/) covers that strategic decision.

## What about after elimination?

Editions differ. Under the Pact’s general procedure, a player voted out reveals their card and stops communicating. In [Stellar Factory’s rules](https://playwerewolf.co/pages/rules), the night victim keeps their character secret. Do not automatically carry a habit from one edition into another.

Decide before the game what will be shown and who will announce it. If an identity stays hidden, do not ask the eliminated player to confirm an accusation with a smile or a nod. Save their explanation for the end, while respecting any special powers in your rules. Our [basic rules guide](/en/blog/how-to-play-werewolf/) puts these steps in the night, discussion and voting cycle.

## Playing around a phone

When using an app, keep private information on the screen out of sight and follow the game’s instructions. Explain the shared rules before assigning roles: a question asked at that point cannot hint at the questioner’s secret identity.
