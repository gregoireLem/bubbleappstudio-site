**Allow about half an hour for The Werewolves of Miller’s Hollow.** That’s the playing time [the publisher lists for the base game](https://www.zygomatic-games.com/game/the-werewolves-of-millers-hollow/). Leave room to teach new players and deal out secret roles. If you use Werewolf Undercover, the phone narrates, but it’s hard to predict how long your friends will want to argue their case before a vote.

## How long is one round?

People use “a round” to mean either a whole game or a single night and the day that follows. To time the shorter cycle at your table, start when everyone closes their eyes. Stop once the next day’s vote is settled. That includes the night actions, the conversation **and** the vote. The full game lasts until a team meets its win condition. See the [Werewolf rules](/en/blog/how-to-play-werewolf/) for when to check.

The first cycle can be slow while people learn when to wake up. A late vote can take just as long for a different reason. With three players left, everyone may want to hear exactly why someone changed their vote the day before.

## Can we start at 8:10 if someone has to leave at 8:45?

That gives you **35 minutes**. Suppose you set aside five minutes to explain and hand out roles. If a night, discussion and vote together took eight minutes, three such cycles would take 24 minutes. Leave five minutes for the reveal and for everyone to grab their coats. That puts you at **8:44**. With a fourth cycle at the same pace, you’d be leaving at **8:52**.

Eight minutes is an example for doing the maths. You don’t know in advance which vote will settle the game. If someone really must leave at 8:45, give them the option to watch this one, or pick a time when everyone can stay for the reveal.

## Make room for the conversation that matters

Explain your edition’s tie rule before handing out roles. Otherwise the first tied vote can turn into a discussion about the rules just as two players are trying to defend themselves. With newcomers, a [practice vote before the game](/en/blog/host-werewolf-first-time-players/) lets everyone try the procedure while nothing is at stake.

If the same accusation keeps coming back, ask, “Does anyone have a question we haven’t answered before we vote?” Give the accused player a chance to reply. When friends want an immediate rematch, use the time from the first deal to the final reveal as your best estimate for this particular group.
