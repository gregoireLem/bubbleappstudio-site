**Two wolves and two villagers do not automatically produce the same result in every edition.** In [Stellar Factory’s rules](https://playwerewolf.co/pages/rules), equal numbers give the wolves a win. In [the Pact rulebook, page 7, in French](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-asmodeeca/uploads/2023/07/WerewolvesThePact_FR_Rules.pdf#page=7), their general objective is to eliminate the other inhabitants. Know which rule you are using before declaring the game over.

## Equal teams: compare the two conditions

This table assumes two teams, no special objectives and all required effects resolved. “Village” means the team opposing the wolves, not just players holding the ordinary Villager card.

| Actual surviving players | Stellar Factory’s rule | The Pact’s general objectives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two wolves and two village-team members | Wolves win at equal numbers. | This count alone does not meet the wolves’ objective. |
| No wolves and two village-team members | Village wins. | Village wins. |

These numbers illustrate an ending; they are not suggested starting setups. For the full game sequence, see our [beginner’s rules guide](/en/blog/how-to-play-werewolf/).

## The moderator’s count is not public information

Imagine four survivors: Lina, Sam, Nora and Paul. The moderator’s notes show that Lina and Sam are wolves. Around the table, players may still wrongly suspect Nora. The result depends on the actual identities, not the majority’s opinion about them.

With a parity win rule, the moderator announces the wolves’ victory when that condition is met. Under the Pact’s general objective, the moderator does not declare a win for that reason alone. They follow the next steps required by the rules without revealing their private count to justify the decision.

A player saying “There must be two wolves left” during discussion is making a claim. The moderator should not turn it into confirmed information. Our [printable moderator sheet](/en/blog/printable-werewolf-moderator-sheet/) helps separate identities known to the moderator from information announced to the group.

## Three checks before announcing a winner

1. **The chosen condition.** Check your edition’s objective: reaching equal numbers and eliminating the opposition are different requirements.
2. **Survivors and their current teams.** A character with a special power can belong to the village. Do not leave them out simply because their card is not called “Villager.”
3. **Any exceptions in play.** An individual objective, team change or consequence of an elimination can affect the result. Follow your rules’ resolution order before concluding. Our [Cupid and lovers guide](/en/blog/werewolf-cupid-lovers/) explores one such case.

Explain these points before dealing roles. At the decisive moment, the group should not be choosing a new rule based on which team seems likely to win.
