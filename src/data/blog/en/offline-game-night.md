**You can play Werewolf without Internet using paper roles and a human moderator.** If you want an app to narrate instead, test it with no connection on the phone you’ll actually use before you leave. An app being designed for people in the same room does not guarantee that every feature works offline.

## Set things up while you still have a connection

Install the game before your trip. For Werewolf Undercover, use the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6740009341) or [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=xyz.bubbleappstudio.werewolfundercover). Our [Android and iPhone installation guide](/en/blog/download-werewolf-android-iphone/) helps you find the correct listing.

Open the app, choose your language and check the roles you intend to use. The store listings describe narration for a group playing together; they do not guarantee that the entire app works without Internet. Plan a rehearsal and pack a backup that does not rely on the phone.

## Test with both mobile data and Wi-Fi off

1. Switch on airplane mode, then check in Settings that Wi-Fi is off too. Airplane mode alone is not proof that the phone is disconnected: [Google explains that Wi-Fi can stay on](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/12639358?hl=en).
2. Reopen the app and run a practice game using your planned language and roles. Go beyond the opening screen: check the instructions, narration and transitions between phases through to the end.
3. If you plan to use a speaker, include it in the test. Also try the phone’s own speaker to help distinguish an audio-output problem from something else.

A successful rehearsal tells you about that particular setup. Test again if you change the phone, app version or language. If the app asks for a connection during the rehearsal, note when it happens. Finding that out at home is easier than discovering it after dealing roles at a cabin. Restore your usual connection settings when you finish testing.

## Pack a backup you can use at the table

Bring matching slips of paper, a pen and the rules for your chosen variant. Ask one person to moderate without joining either team. Our [original Werewolf moderator script](/en/blog/werewolf-moderator-script/) offers a simple version with only Werewolves and ordinary Villagers, including explicit tie-breaking and victory rules.

Print the script or save a copy you can read without a connection. Check that it opens while disconnected; keeping a bookmark alone is not enough. Fold the role slips alike and keep the moderator’s notes out of sight.

Imagine arriving at a holiday cottage with no signal. Your phone did not pass the offline test, but Jo is happy to moderate the first game. You take out the slips and script, agree on the rules and deal fresh roles. If you had already started an app-led game, avoid revealing everyone’s secret information while trying to reconstruct it. Start a new game together instead.
