**Getting the most votes does not always mean a player has enough votes to be eliminated.** For a Mafia or Werewolf game night, check your version’s voting procedure before assigning roles. With seven people voting, three votes might put someone in first place, but they are not more than half.

This guide covers running a vote when friends play in the same room. Start with our [Werewolf rules](/en/blog/how-to-play-werewolf/) for teams and phases. If your group usually plays Mafia, the [Mafia vs Werewolf comparison](/en/blog/mafia-vs-werewolf/) explains which differences to check.

## Settle five questions before the first vote

- **Who can vote?** Count the players entitled to vote under the roles in play. A moderator outside the teams does not become an extra voter.
- **What is the choice?** Eliminating one nominated person, or choosing among several suspects?
- **What result is enough?** More than half of a defined total, or the highest number of votes?
- **When is a choice final?** Explain the signal and how players should keep their choices visible while you count.
- **What happens without a decision?** Check ties, any permitted abstentions and the end of discussion time.

If a power changes a vote’s weight or a player’s voting rights, explain the general effect without revealing who holds the role. The examples below assume one vote per person and no special powers.

## Three out of seven: majority or plurality?

Imagine this fictional tally:

| Player receiving votes | Votes |
| --- | --- |
| Alex | 3 |
| Sam | 2 |
| Jordan | 2 |

Alex has the most votes: a plurality. **If your rule requires more than half of all seven votes, the threshold is four**, so nobody has reached it. If your rule eliminates the player with the sole highest count, Alex is out. The same tally can produce different outcomes under different rules.

Check the denominator too: all eligible voters, or votes actually cast? An abstention does not automatically lower the threshold. Settle that interpretation before seeing the results.

## A nomination vote is different from choosing among suspects

In [Stellar Factory’s rules](https://playwerewolf.co/pages/rules), an accusation backed by a second player leads to a defense, then a majority vote on elimination. Without that majority, discussion resumes.

That is different from having everyone point at their preferred suspect simultaneously. A nomination can fail without another suspect automatically being eliminated.

Our [moderator script for a homemade game](/en/blog/werewolf-moderator-script/) offers a different framework: each player points to another remaining player, and the highest count decides. Those are the house rules stated in that script, not universal Mafia rules or a promise about how the app operates.

## What happens in a tied vote?

**Use the tie rule you explained before dealing roles.** Six votes split three to three do not produce a unique leader. Neither side has more than half of the six votes, either.

Under the house rules in our script, the remaining players vote again between the tied candidates. Players still choose someone other than themselves. If the runoff is tied again, nobody is eliminated that day. This is a variant to agree on at the start.

Do not suddenly replace that procedure with a coin toss, an extra moderator vote or eliminating both candidates. If the group forgot to cover ties, consult your chosen rules before continuing the count.

## Count aloud without giving hints

For a simultaneous vote with fixed choices, announce the signal first, then ask everyone to keep their choice visible while you count. Here is an original line for an example with seven votes and an agreed threshold of four:

> “Keep your choices visible. Alex has four votes, Sam has two, and Jordan has one. That accounts for all seven. The threshold is four, so the vote selects Alex.”

If a gesture is hidden, clarify it before announcing the result. Avoid asking just one group of voters, “Are you really sure?” That can sound like a hint from the moderator.

After the vote, resolve any relevant powers and role reveals under your version, then check the [win conditions](/en/blog/werewolf-win-conditions/) before starting another night.
