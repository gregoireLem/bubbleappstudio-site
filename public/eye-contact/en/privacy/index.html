Eye Contact: Photo Timelapse

Privacy

2026-10-21

Your photos stay with you

Eye Contact processes photos on your iPhone. We do not collect or receive your photos, eye positions, face information, projects or usage analytics. There is no account, advertising or tracking SDK.

Faces and eye positions

Apple Vision locates faces and eyes to align your photos. Face boxes, eye coordinates and your manual corrections are saved with the project on your iPhone. They are not used to identify you or sent to a server. Eye Contact cannot access the identities in Photos’ People & Pets collection.

Photos access

The photo picker shares only the images you select. Importing an album requires Photos access. Saving a film requests permission to add it to Photos. You can manage these permissions in Settings. Photos stored in iCloud may be downloaded by iOS.

Storage and deletion

Projects, imported photo copies and generated films are stored in the app. Deleting a project removes its cached photos when no other project uses them. Original photos and videos saved to Photos are kept. Deleting the app removes its local data. Your device backups may contain app data; manage those backups in your Apple settings.

Purchases

Apple processes payments and restores purchases through StoreKit. Eye Contact uses the verified purchase status to unlock exports and does not receive your payment card details. Apple’s services operate under Apple’s privacy policy.
