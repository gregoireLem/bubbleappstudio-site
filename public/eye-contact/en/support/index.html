Eye Contact: Photo Timelapse

Support

Import your photos

1. Open Photos → Collections → People & Pets → your face.

2. Show all photos, select the ones you want and add them to an album, such as “My film”.

In Eye Contact, tap Import an album and choose the album you prepared.

Adjust photo

If there are several faces, choose yours. If the eyes are not detected correctly, mark them with two taps or exclude the photo.

Projects

Your edits are saved automatically on this iPhone. Duplicate a project to try another version.

Restore purchases

To restore the lifetime purchase, open the export screen and tap Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. If already unlocked, exporting opens the finished film directly.
